## Hainanese Chicken Rice (Lighter Version)

All the flavour of the hawker classic — silky chicken, fragrant rice, sharp chilli — but with the skin off and much less oil. Your heart also happy.

### Ingredients
**Chicken**
* 3 skinless chicken thighs (bone-in)
* 2 litres water
* 1 tsp salt
* 5 cm ginger, sliced
* 2 cloves garlic, smashed
* 2 stalks spring onion
* 1 star anise
* A large bowl of ice water

**Rice**
* 300 g jasmine rice (or half brown rice), washed and drained
* 1 tsp neutral oil
* 1 shallot, 4 cloves garlic and 5 cm ginger, finely chopped
* 1 pandan leaf, knotted (optional)
* 450 ml chicken poaching stock, fat skimmed off

**Chilli sauce**
* 4 red bird's eye chillies
* 3 cm ginger and 3 cloves garlic
* 1 tsp sugar and ¼ tsp salt
* 80 ml chicken poaching stock
* 1 tsp rice vinegar

**To serve**
* 1 cucumber, sliced
* Light soy sauce (a little)

### Instructions
1. Take the chicken out of the fridge 1 hour before cooking so it poaches evenly.
2. Bring the water, salt, ginger, garlic, spring onion and star anise to the boil in a large pot.
3. Lower in the chicken thighs, reduce to a gentle simmer — small bubbles only — and poach for 15 minutes.
4. Move the chicken straight into the ice water for 10 minutes so the meat stays juicy and tender.
5. Let the stock cool for a few minutes, then skim the fat off the surface with a spoon.
6. Heat the oil in a non-stick pot, fry the shallot, garlic and ginger for 1–2 minutes until fragrant, then stir in the drained rice.
7. Add 450 ml of the skimmed stock and the pandan leaf. Bring to the boil, then cover and cook on the lowest heat for 12 minutes (add 5 minutes if using brown rice). Rest, covered, for 10 minutes.
8. For the chilli sauce, pound or blend the chillies, ginger, garlic, sugar and salt, then stir in the stock and vinegar.
9. Slice the chicken and serve with the rice, plenty of cucumber and the chilli sauce. Season the remaining skimmed stock with white pepper and serve it as soup.

### Tips
* Skimming the stock removes most of the fat — the flavour stays.
* Chicken breast works too; poach it for 10–12 minutes only, or it goes dry.
* Go easy on the soy sauce and chilli salt if you're also watching sodium.
